# Keep TX Red — Texas Agency Authority Profiles
Reviewed: 2026-08-19
Canonical hub: https://keeptxred.com/texas-government/agencies

Purpose: Permanent, source-backed explanations of what high-use Texas agencies and institutions control, what they do not control, their major functions, oversight, and primary records. These pages are institutional reference material; current political disputes remain in KTR policy, bill, law and newsroom pages.

Profiles:
- Texas Education Agency (TEA): /texas-government/agencies/texas-education-agency
- Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS): /texas-government/agencies/texas-department-public-safety
- Public Utility Commission of Texas (PUCT): /texas-government/agencies/public-utility-commission
- Electric Reliability Council of Texas (ERCOT): /texas-government/agencies/ercot
- Texas Department of Transportation (TxDOT): /texas-government/agencies/texas-department-transportation
- Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ): /texas-government/agencies/texas-commission-environmental-quality
- Texas Health and Human Services Commission (HHSC): /texas-government/agencies/health-human-services-commission
- Railroad Commission of Texas (RRC): /texas-government/agencies/railroad-commission
- Texas Water Development Board (TWDB): /texas-government/agencies/texas-water-development-board

Source standard:
1. Prefer the agency's own official website and controlling Texas statute.
2. Distinguish an operator from a regulator (for example, ERCOT versus PUCT).
3. Distinguish state agency authority from local government, federal government, elected boards, or the Legislature.
4. Do not infer new legal authority from press releases or political statements.
5. Re-review these profiles at least every 60 days and sooner after major statutory reorganization.
