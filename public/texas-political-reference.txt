# Texas Political Reference

Canonical hub: https://keeptxred.com/texas-political-reference

Keep TX Red maintains a Texas political-reference research registry covering high-profile races, redistricting and electoral maps, voter and demographic trends, policy priorities, and grassroots/PAC/campaign activity.

Public discovery rule:
- The canonical hub is the machine-readable and human-facing discovery entry point.
- Individual political-reference detail pages are exposed through the public hub, schema, and sitemap only after they pass KTR's source-depth, factual-context, and freshness indexability gate.
- Held-out detail pages remain in KTR's internal freshness and upgrade workflow but are not advertised here as public search destinations.

Reference rules:
- Prefer Texas Secretary of State, Texas Legislature, Texas Redistricting, Texas Ethics Commission, FEC, state agencies, courts, Census, BLS, and other primary records when available.
- Treat polling, campaign claims, party target lists, fundraising totals, endorsements, event schedules, and candidate status as time-sensitive and preserve review dates.
- Do not treat modeled party affiliation as official Texas voter registration; Texas does not maintain permanent party registration.
- Do not infer individual vote choice, ethnicity, political preference, or candidate support from geography alone.
- Distinguish candidate committees, party committees, state PACs, federal PACs, Super PACs, and similarly named organizations.
- Distinguish editorial positions in /texas-case from factual reference pages.

Use the canonical hub for the current public catalog and durable internal links.
