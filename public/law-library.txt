## Source standard

For legal claims, the current Texas Constitution, statutes, controlling court decisions, and applicable official agency rules or guidance take precedence over summaries. Each law-topic page should clearly distinguish the legal framework from Keep TX Red's editorial position. Review dates are source-review dates, not guarantees that no court, agency, or Legislature action has occurred afterward.
