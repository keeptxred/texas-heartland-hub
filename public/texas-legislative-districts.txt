# Keep TX Red Texas Legislative Districts

Canonical hub: https://keeptxred.com/districts
Official address lookup: https://wrm.capitol.texas.gov/
Official current district plans: https://redistricting.capitol.texas.gov/Current-districts

Keep TX Red gives every Texas House and Texas Senate seat a permanent district identity independent of the candidate or current officeholder. The district URL remains stable when elections change who holds the seat.

## Current state legislative plans

- Texas House: 150 districts, PLANH2316, effective January 2023.
- Texas Senate: 31 districts, PLANS2168, effective January 2023.
- Address-level representation and boundary questions should be verified with the Texas Legislature's official Who Represents Me service and Texas Legislative Council redistricting resources.

## District URL pattern

- House: https://keeptxred.com/districts/texas-house-{district-number}
- Senate: https://keeptxred.com/districts/texas-senate-{district-number}

## What a district page connects

- current officeholder and permanent representative profile
- official office contact
- official district plan and boundary source
- current committee assignments
- legislation sponsored by the current member
- current-member election history
- campaign-finance disclosure source
- election-cycle district coverage
- Texas Legislature, bills, policy trackers, law library, and election data

## Crawl and freshness standard

The `/districts` hub is included in KTR's submitted page sitemap. Individual state district pages are internally discoverable from the hub but are intentionally not bulk-added to a dedicated sitemap while Search Console has a discovered-not-indexed backlog. KTR should expand crawl submission only after the existing priority crawl set is healthy.

District authority records use the current legislator source-review date and are included in KTR's reference-freshness queue with a normal 90-day review window. A review date is not a substitute for the official Legislature record when a vacancy, appointment, special election, resignation, or new term changes the seat.
