# Political figure current-fact notes

Evergreen profile copy in the 100-leader authority cluster intentionally corrects stale source-list role labels where the office or committee changed.

Key examples as of August 26, 2026:

- Glenn Hegar is a former Texas comptroller and has served as chancellor of the Texas A&M University System since July 1, 2025.
- Nathan Hecht retired from the Supreme Court of Texas effective December 31, 2024.
- James D. Blacklock became chief justice of the Supreme Court of Texas in January 2025.
- Dade Phelan is a former Speaker of the Texas House; Dustin Burrows became speaker in January 2025.
- Bryan Hughes chairs Senate State Affairs.
- Joan Huffman chairs Senate Finance; the Senate Jurisprudence Committee was dissolved in the September 2025 committee reorganization.
- Paul Bettencourt chairs Senate Local Government.

The evergreen pages avoid making temporary committee assignments the core SEO title where doing so would make the profile stale after a future reorganization.
