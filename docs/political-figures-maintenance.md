# Political figure maintenance rules

- Preserve one canonical profile URL per person.
- Put aliases in profile metadata rather than creating duplicate person pages.
- Keep career history and institutional context on evergreen profiles.
- Put live candidacy, ballot, polling, and result facts in Election Central.
- Prefer durable role wording when committee assignments can change between sessions or Congresses.
- Keep the 100-name target test passing when profile copy or routes are refactored.
- Do not remove established non-target political profiles merely to force the overall route registry to 100; the public target collection itself is the exact 100-name list.
