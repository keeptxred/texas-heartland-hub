# October 13, 2026 San Antonio environmental review lawsuit — source ledger

A new federal case was filed October 13 in the Western District of Texas: case 5:26-cv-06542. City of San Antonio alleges the federal agencies did not properly review conversion of the approximately 640,000-square-foot East Side warehouse for a planned 1,500-person detention facility. Claims are not court findings.
