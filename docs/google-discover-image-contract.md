# Google Discover image contract

Keep TX Red article hero images generated by the Cloudflare image pipeline must use a large landscape canvas suitable for Google Discover.

## Production contract

- Preferred generated size: **1280×720 (16:9)**.
- Generated article images must stay on a path that can request at least **1200 pixels of width**.
- The preferred article image must be relevant to the story and remain the image supplied through Open Graph and `NewsArticle` metadata.
- `max-image-preview:large` must remain enabled on indexable article pages.
- Low-resolution or square fallback generation must not silently replace the large 16:9 production image path.
- A future model swap must update the regression tests before changing these dimensions.

This contract tracks Google Search Central's Discover guidance and exists so future image-model changes do not accidentally regress article imagery below the large-image recommendation.
