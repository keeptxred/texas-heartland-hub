# Business formation and LLC governance — bounded no-change primary-source review

**Date:** October 29, 2026. **Guide:** `src/data/law-topic-business-formation-authority.ts`. **Disposition:** the selected formation/governance claims already use accurate qualified language. **No guide text, reviewed date, published date or site metadata changed.**

## Official legal authorities checked

- [Texas Business Organizations Code Chapter 3](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/BO/htm/BO.3.htm), **§3.001(a)–(d)**: a filing entity is formed by a certificate filed under Chapter 4; its existence begins when the filing **takes effect**, and the issued filing acknowledgment has specified conclusive evidentiary effects. The guide correctly distinguishes submission from acceptance/effectiveness. **§3.002**: the formation of nonfiling entities depends on their applicable Code title, not necessarily the LLC/corporate Form 205 process.
- [Texas Business Organizations Code Chapter 4](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/Docs/BO/htm/BO.4.htm), **§§4.051–.053**: filing effectiveness and statutory conditions for delayed effective dates/events; the guide accurately says some filings can have delayed effectiveness. It makes no unsupported blanket claim that any organizer may unilaterally choose an unlimited effective date.
- [Texas Business Organizations Code Chapter 101](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/BO/htm/BO.101.htm), **§101.001(1)**: company agreements may be written, implied or oral as specified; a one-member LLC agreement is not unenforceable merely because one party is involved. **§101.101(a)–(c)**: LLCs may have one or more members, with tightly described exceptional no-member transition periods; the guide's single-member FAQ is accurate and does not state that zero members is a general permanent normal arrangement.
- **§§101.052–.054**: company agreements govern specified internal affairs subject to listed statutory limits/nonwaivable rules. The existing framework's qualified sentence about statute, management and agreements together is appropriate.

## Unchanged editorial outcome

The guide's introductory filing-versus-nonfiling distinction, statement about effectiveness and delayed filings, one-member FAQ, and company-agreement framework were source-consistent. No new public copy is warranted. Retain existing `updated` and `published` values of **October 23, 2026** because only selected claims were reviewed.
