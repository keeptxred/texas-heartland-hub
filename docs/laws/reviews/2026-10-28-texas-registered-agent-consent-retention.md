# Texas LLC registered agent: consent retention, optional filing and real office

**Reviewed:** October 28–29, 2026. **Guide:** `src/data/law-topic-business-formation-authority.ts` / `/laws/topic/business-formation-llc-law`.

## Official source findings

- [Texas Business Organizations Code Chapter 5, current text](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/BO/htm/BO.5.htm):
  - **§5.201(a):** A filing entity and each foreign filing entity must designate and **continuously maintain** a Texas registered agent and registered office.
  - **§5.201(b):** Agent must be a consenting resident individual or eligible authorized organization **other than the filing entity itself**. Consent is required in a proper **written or electronic form**, and the agent must have a business office at the registered office.
  - **§5.201(c):** The registered office requires a physical **street address where process may be personally served**; it is not necessarily the entity's main business address and may **not** solely be a mailbox service or telephone answering service.
- [Texas Secretary of State, Form 401-A instructions](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp/instructions/401-a.shtml): **written/electronic agent consent is mandatory but filing the signed consent with SOS is generally not**. The entity ordinarily receives and retains the agent's signed consent. Separate public filing of the consent is optional with a fee, not a condition of ordinary LLC formation. Form 401-A is an acceptable evidence form, but the SOS does not insist consent use only that printed form.
- [Texas Secretary of State, Registered Agents FAQs](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp/registeredagentfaqs.shtml): an agent may reject a designation made without consent; changes to appointed agent or official service address should be filed with SOS as appropriate using Form 401. Lack of ongoing agent/office may result in domestic entity termination or foreign authority revocation.
- [FinCEN BOI home page](https://www.fincen.gov/boi) and [August 11, 2026 final-rule press release](https://www.fincen.gov/news/news-releases/fincen-permanently-ends-beneficial-ownership-reporting-requirements-millions) independently support existing guide's correct distinction: the final **effective August 14, 2026** permanently exempted **U.S.-created companies and U.S persons** from the BOI reporting requirement, while certain foreign-organized companies registered in the US remain covered. That federal reporting status does not change Texas registered-agent obligations.
