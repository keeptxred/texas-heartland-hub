# Texas parental-rights education: SB 12, SB 204, board portal and Proposition 15 — bounded no-change source review

**Reviewed:** October 28–29, 2026. **Guide:** `src/data/law-topic-parental-rights-education-authority.ts` / `/laws/topic/parental-rights-education-law`. Public guide `updated: 2026-09-06` unchanged.

## Primary authorities and observed law

1. [Current Texas Education Code Chapter 26, official statutory text](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/ED/htm/ED.26.htm) contains **two independently enacted sections numbered §26.0025**, with reciprocal cross-references:
   - **89(R) SB 204, Chapter 965** added **§26.0025, Parental Rights Handbook**, effective September 1, 2025. TEA must produce a **plain-language** handbook about a child's education rights, **update it at least annually**, and make it publicly available **searchably** online.
   - **89(R) SB 12, Chapter 1125** separately added **§26.0025, Right to Select Educational Setting**, also effective September 1, 2025, including parent choice among public, private and home school. The duplicated official numbering is not an excuse to merge unrelated legal rights or presume one provision silently repealed the other.
2. [Texas Education Code §26.0071, official current text](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/GetStatute.aspx?Code=ED&Value=26.0071), added by SB 12 effective September 1, 2025, requires each **school district board of trustees** to develop a parental-engagement policy: an online portal for comments to campus/district administrators or board, public comments presented **at the beginning** of each board meeting, and meetings **outside typical work hours**. This duty is addressed to covered district boards, not every private school; lawfully closed meetings under §26.007 remain distinct.
3. [Texas Education Agency — Required Trustee Training and Handbook Available (2026)](https://tea.texas.gov/taa-letters/required-trustee-parental-rights-training-and-parental-rights-handbook-available) confirms **TEA's handbook has actually been released** and that SB 12's **Parental Rights and Options Information Form** must be provided when a child first enrolls, again at the start of each school year, and posted conspicuously on the district website. The form is a notification mechanism, not proof a specific individual right was violated.
4. [Texas Legislative Reference Library — Proposition 15 / SJR 34 election details](https://lrl.texas.gov/legis/billsearch/amendmentdetails.cfm?amendmentID=738&billNumberDetail=34&billSuffixDetail=&billtypeDetail=SJR&legSession=89-0) verifies **adopted November 4, 2025**. [Enrolled SJR 34 final text](https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/89R/billtext/html/SJ00034F.HTM) adds Texas Constitution Article I §37, affirming parents' responsibility to protect/nurture a child and fundamental right of care, custody and control including upbringing decisions. It does **not**, by itself, erase federal privacy statutes or custody court orders.
5. **Chapter 26 §26.002** also defines parent with exclusions for termination/lack of possession or access under court order, and qualified educational rights of adult/emancipated students; the current guide's caveats about different student/guardian legal statuses remain appropriate.

## Result

**No substantive guide correction warranted for these bounded claims.** The existing guide already accurately flags **two distinct §26.0025 provisions**, distinguishes SB 204 handbook from SB 12 form and board comments, correctly identifies the constitutional amendment as *adopted* rather than proposed, and does not falsely promise access to other children's protected records. Preserve the original **2026-09-06** public update date. Document this only in the internal source ledger.

**Still unreviewed:** Each health-consent/screening right and emergency exception, all public vs charter vs private applications, particular FERPA/IDEA/Section504 fact patterns, election/grievance pathways, actual individual district policy compliance, subsequent TEA rules and the rest of the guide. This is not a full legal source certification or advice in a particular dispute. Protected PR CI/merge and exact-main production evidence are separate.
