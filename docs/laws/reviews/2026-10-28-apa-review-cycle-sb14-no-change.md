# Texas Administrative Procedure Act — bounded official-source no-change review

**Reviewed:** October 28–29, 2026. **Guide:** `src/data/law-topic-administrative-rulemaking-authority.ts` / `/laws/topic/administrative-rulemaking`. The guide retains original `updated: "2026-09-06"`.

## Controlling sources, reviewed claims

1. [Current Texas Government Code, Chapter 2001, official codified law](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/GV/htm/GV.2001.htm)
   - **§2001.036(a):** An adopted state agency rule ordinarily becomes effective **20 days after filing** with the Secretary of State, with statutory exceptions for later effective dates, qualifying emergency findings, and required federal deadlines. Thus an adopted rule is not necessarily effective the day a board votes or publishes a notice. The page's framework, key rule and effective-date FAQ correctly preserve that distinction.
   - **§2001.039(a)-(e):** The agency must review each rule by the **fourth anniversary of original effect** and every four years after; an amendment alone does **not** reset the date unless statutory rule review occurred formally as part of the amendment process. Resulting readoption/repeal requires the applicable agency procedure. The page states this distinction, without suggesting a review notice automatically repeals a rule.
   - **§2001.035(a)-(d):** The two-year time limit applies to challenges alleging noncompliance with specifically listed **rule-adoption procedural requirements**, and does not itself impose two years on *all possible legal challenges*; substantial compliance and material prejudice matter. The guide limits this language to procedural challenges.
   - **§2001.038:** A permitted declaratory action regarding rule validity/applicability involves a legal right allegedly impaired or threatened, the **Travis County district court**, and joining the agency. The page is limited to this cause of action, not all agency disputes.
2. [Enrolled 89th Legislature SB 14, 2025 (final law), sections 7-11](https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/89R/billtext/html/SB00014F.HTM)
   - **§2001.042:** Courts are not required to defer to an agency's legal reading; reasonable interpretations that do not contradict the statute's plain language can still be considered.
   - **§2001.1721:** De novo review of the legal issues in proceedings covered by that section with the same allowance for reasonable agency legal views.
   - **Section 10 applicability:** Amendments to §§2001.024, 2001.035, 2001.040 and specified repeals apply to **rules first proposed on or after September 1, 2025**; older proposals continue under former law for that purpose.
   - **Section 11 applicability:** The new §§2001.042 and 2001.1721 apply only to **specified proceedings initiated on or after September 1, 2025**, with earlier proceedings governed by the former law.
