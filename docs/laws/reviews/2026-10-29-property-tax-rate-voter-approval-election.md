**Scope:** `src/data/law-topic-property-tax-authority.ts` truth-in-taxation paragraph, one key rule, one new FAQ, two official source links. **Finding:** the previous general description is correct but leaves material automatic-election vs petition and school-district distinctions unclear.

## Primary law and agency guidance

- [Texas Tax Code Chapter 26](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/TX/htm/TX.26.htm): §26.04 distinguishes no-new-revenue and voter-approval rate calculations, and statutory formula differences for a special taxing unit versus others; school districts have separate legislation and rate-calculation requirements.
- [Texas Comptroller Truth-in-Taxation](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/truth-in-taxation/): no-new-revenue uses comparable existing property, not a cap on each parcel's bill. Most nonschool nonspecial calculations distinguish no-new-revenue M&O, the ordinary 3.5 percent component, debt service and eligible unused increment. This is not a universal 3.5 percent ceiling on total individual bills.
- [Texas Comptroller Elections to Approve Tax Rate](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/truth-in-taxation/rollback-elections.php): under the applicable §§26.07 and 26.08 rules, most adopted rates above voter approval require automatic November elections; some de minimis conditions allow voter petition, while school-district TRE and special-unit rules require their own statutory analysis.
