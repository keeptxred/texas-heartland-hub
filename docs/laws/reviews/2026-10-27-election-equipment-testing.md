| Chapter 129 voting-system public logic-and-accuracy test | [Election Code §129.023(b), (b-3), (c-1)](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/EL/htm/EL.129.htm) | Ordinarily no later than **48th DAY before election day** with public notice ≥48 HOURS ahead. Enacted **Sept 1 2025**. A few SOS calendar snippets shorthand this as '§129.023', but the **codified governing pollbook statute is §129.0231**, not §129.023(b). |
| Voting-system capability restrictions | [§129.054(a)-(c)](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/EL/htm/EL.129.htm) | As of **Sept 1 2026**, systems covered by Chapter 129 may not have capability to connect to external or internal communication networks or capability for wireless communication. Distinct from electronic pollbook software and public results websites; law is not a universal ban on every election computer. |

Existing date-specific guide language upheld; not changed. |
