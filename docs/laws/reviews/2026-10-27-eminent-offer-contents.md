# Texas eminent domain offers — §21.0113(b) required contents

**Checked:** October 27, 2026. [Master issue #2938](https://github.com/keeptxred/texas-heartland-hub/issues/2938). **Scope:** The written initial and final bona fide acquisition offer components under Texas Property Code §21.0113(b), in the existing eminent-domain guide. This is an additional scoped claim review, **not** comprehensive signoff of that guide or all 18 authority pages.

| Offer stage | Controlling authority | Verified legal requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Initial written offer | [Texas Property Code §21.0113(b)(1)(A)-(D)](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/docs/PR/htm/PR.21.htm); [TxDOT official acquisition manual](https://www.txdot.gov/manuals/row/edm/chapter-2--state-acquisition-by-eminent-domain/section-1--offer-letters/initial-offer.html) | Initial offer must include Landowner's Bill of Rights and specified addendum when applicable; conspicuous **bold, larger-font** notice regarding remaining-property damages or qualifying certified appraisal; proposed conveyance instrument including applicable §21.0114 private-entity safeguards, subject to enumerated exceptions; and the name and telephone of an authorized entity representative. This is more than a nonbinding informal compensation quote. |
| Timing / qualified certified appraisal | [§21.0113(b)(2)-(7)](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/docs/PR/htm/PR.21.htm); [TxDOT bona fide offer checklist](https://www.txdot.gov/manuals/row/acq/right_of_way_property_acquisition/written_offer_to_purchase_required_for_state/elements-of-required--bona-fide-offer-.html) | Final written offer made no sooner than day 30 after initial offer; before making final offer the entity obtains qualified **certified appraiser's** written valuation of property and legally recoverable remainder damages; final offer amount **equal to or exceeding** written appraisal; final package includes previously supplied or attached appraisal, proposed conveyance instrument and Landowner's Bill of Rights. Owner receives >=14 days to respond. These are separate requirements and windows. |
| Penalty for failing bonafide offer / procedural distinction | [§21.047(d)](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/docs/PR/htm/PR.21.htm) | Prior protected guide correction properly requires judicial finding the §21.0113 bona fide offer was not made, then abatement, new compliant offer, qualifying costs and violation-related professional fees. Merely rejecting appraised value does not itself guarantee those remedies. No edit to prior corrected claim. |
