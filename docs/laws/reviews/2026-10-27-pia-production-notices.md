# PIA prompt-production certification and emailed URL access — bounded review

**Reviewed 2026-10-27.** This is a **partial statutory claim review** of Texas Government Code Section 552.221(b-1), (b-2), (d), not a completed legal audit of all provisions in the KeepTXRed Public Information Act guide or all 18 Texas Laws guides.

| Reader question | Primary Texas statute | Finding and editorial treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Can a government agency send a website URL rather than copies? | [Gov. Code §552.221(b-1)](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/GetStatute.aspx?Code=GV&Value=552.221) | Can supply **exact** public-government URL for identifiable and readily available records; merely sending a general homepage does not show requested records are accessible. Existing guide supported; retain. |
| If the officer emails the URL, must the email identify other ways to obtain the information? | [§552.221(b-2)](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/GetStatute.aspx?Code=GV&Value=552.221) | Yes. A **conspicuous** email statement must specify that inspection, duplication, or receipt through United States mail remains available under subsection (b). This protection was omitted from earlier FAQ; add it. |
| Does the body have a universal ten-day deadline to release every record? | [§552.221(d)](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/GetStatute.aspx?Code=GV&Value=552.221); [AG requester overview](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/open-government/members-public/overview-public-information-act) | No. When officer **cannot produce requested information for inspection or duplication within 10 business days** after the request, the officer **shall certify that fact in writing** and set a reasonable **date and hour** for availability. This is more specific than optional informal notification. |
| Does ten-day delayed-record availability excuse failure to seek Attorney General withholding ruling? | [Gov. Code §552.301](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/GetStatute.aspx?Code=GV&Value=552.301) | No. Subchapter G withholding-ruling deadlines are distinct from §552.221(d) written production-delay certification. Do not conflate the two clocks. |
