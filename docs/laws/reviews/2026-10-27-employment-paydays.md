# Texas employment law — payroll frequency and bonus-payment primary-source review

**Checked:** October 27, 2026. **Master tracker:** [#2938](https://github.com/keeptxred/texas-heartland-hub/issues/2938). Scope: regular pay frequency, posted payday default, compensation-agreement commission/bonus timing, and relationship to already accurate final-pay and TWC filing deadlines. **This is bounded; not a full employment guide certification.**

| Claim group | Primary authority | Verified scope and disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Overtime-exempt vs other employee paycheck cadence | [Texas Labor Code §61.011(a)–(c)](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/LA/htm/LA.61.htm), [TWC Frequency of Pay](https://efte.twc.texas.gov/frequency_of_pay.html) | Covered employees exempt from federal FLSA overtime: no less than monthly. Other employees: no less than twice monthly. For twice-monthly payroll, periods should contain nearly equal numbers of days. A salary title does not prove an FLSA overtime exemption. **Added explicit framework, key rule, FAQ and regression.** |
| Designated and posted paydays | [§61.012(a)–(c)](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/LA/htm/LA.61.htm), [TWC Frequency of Pay](https://efte.twc.texas.gov/frequency_of_pay.html) | Employer must designate compliant paydays and post workplace notice. If none designated, the **first and fifteenth** of each month apply. Not an absolute rule requiring all employers to pay on those dates; this is the **statutory default only**. Added precise FAQ and rule. |
| Commission and bonus compensation terms | [§61.015(a)–(b)](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/LA/htm/LA.61.htm), [TWC Final Pay — Severance](https://efte.twc.texas.gov/final_pay_severance_benefits.html) | When commission/bonus is **due** is governed by employment agreement or applicable collective bargaining agreement, subject to applicable Payday Law timely payment. Distinguish final ordinary wages, deferred commission accrual and voluntary/involuntary separation. Added explicit qualification; **do not promise instant post-termination payout irrespective of plan**. |
| Final regular wages; TWC wage claim | [§61.014(a)–(b) and §61.051(c)](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/LA/htm/LA.61.htm) | Involuntary separation generally six calendar days, voluntary separation next regular payday; TWC unpaid wage claim within 180th day of **when wages became due**, jurisdictional. Existing guide text correct and retained. |
| State employment discrimination and sexual-harassment employer coverage/periods | [Labor Code §§21.141, 21.142, 21.202](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/LA/htm/LA.21.htm) | Sexual-harassment employer standard includes one or more employees; state complaint 300-day sexual-harassment filing vs generally 180-day other Chapter 21 conduct; immediate appropriate corrective action when known or should have known. Existing guide correctly separates these. |
| Worker classification; minimum wage, at-will | [DOL FLSA employment relationship fact sheet](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fact-sheets/13-flsa-employment-relationship), [DOL state minimum-wage chart](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/minimum-wage/state), [TWC general Texas pay-policy guide](https://efte.twc.texas.gov/pay_and_policies_general.html) | Labels alone do not determine independent-contractor or overtime exempt status; federal and state coverage distinct. Texas state adopts federal minimum by reference, FLSA coverage varies. At-will is general default absent exception/agreement, not exclusion of wage/discrimination claims. Existing narrative cautious; not an individualized classification finding. |
