# Texas driving/vehicle registration: Bexar County emissions effective November 1, 2026

**Reviewed:** October 28–29, 2026. **Guide:** `src/data/law-topic-driving-traffic-authority.ts` / `/laws/topic/driving-traffic-insurance-law`.

## Primary implementation sources

- [Texas DPS — Bexar County Emissions Inspection FAQs, Feb. 10, 2026](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/vehicle-inspection/faq/bexar-county-emission-inspections) confirms **November 1, 2026** program commencement. For Bexar County gasoline-powered vehicles **2–24 years old**, a passing emissions test is required as a condition of applicable registration, subject to agency/statuory exemptions and processes. It is an emissions inspection, not a reinstated general noncommercial safety inspection.
- [Texas DPS — March 2026 emissions inspection criteria](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/vehicle-inspection/inspection-criteria-emission-inspection) identifies the affected vehicle/fuel/age criteria and distinguishes Bexar's added November start from other counties with existing testing.
- [TxDMV — October 9, 2026 official registration reminder](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/TXDMV/bulletins/42e59be) confirms covered 2–24-year gasoline vehicles registered in Bexar must meet the test before initial registration or renewal **starting November 1**; a registration can be renewed **up to 90 days before expiry**, and the emissions inspection for that renewal must occur within its 90-day period. A vehicle with a still-valid registration need not test on the first program day merely because the program started.
- [Bexar County Tax Assessor-Collector motor vehicle page](https://www.bexar.org/1601/Motor-Vehicle) independently confirms the scheduled November 1 start and distinguishes emissions from vehicle registration.
- [Texas DPS — Jan. 3, 2025 HB 3297 implementation](https://www.dps.texas.gov/news/vehicle-safety-inspection-program-changes-now-effect) states noncommercial safety inspection requirement generally ended January 1, 2025, while county emissions and commercial safety inspections continue, with a replacement fee for covered noncommercial registration.
