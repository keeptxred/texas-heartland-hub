# Texas November 3, 2026 election deadlines — source-backed no-change review

**Checked:** October 28, 2026 (Central time). **Guide:** `src/data/law-topic-election-authority.ts` / `/laws/topic/election-law`. **Public guide date remains September 6, 2026.**

## Controlling official election guidance checked

- [Texas Secretary of State — November 3, 2026 Election Law Calendar (Advisory 2026-21)](https://www.sos.texas.gov/elections/laws/advisory2026-21-november-3-election-law-calendar.shtml) and [Important Election Dates](https://www.sos.texas.gov/elections/voter/important-election-dates.shtml):
  - Early voting by personal appearance: **Monday October 19 through Friday October 30, 2026**.
  - Application for ballot by mail (**ABBM**) must be **received**, not merely postmarked, by **Friday October 23, 2026** (now past). An emailed/faxed application ordinarily still needs the original received by the fourth business day under the separate requirement.
  - Election Day **Tuesday November 3, 2026**, ordinary polling hours **7 a.m. to 7 p.m.**, with local polling place and line-closing rules.
  - Normal domestic mail-ballot carrier envelope: received **by 7 p.m. November 3**; if timely postmarked/placed for delivery by that time, the qualifying domestic late-arrival exception permits receipt by **5 p.m. Wednesday November 4**. These are **ballot return**, not new ABBM request, deadlines; overseas, military and emergency scenarios have different rules.
  - Certain carrier-envelope defects can be corrected **in person through Monday November 9**, the sixth day after election day, subject to Sections 87.0271 and 87.0411. This does not guarantee every missing ballot or defect is remediable.
- [Texas SOS — Important dates for primary, convention and general elections](https://www.sos.texas.gov/elections/candidates/guide/2026/dates2026.shtml) independently confirms October 19, 23, 30 and November 3 and distinguishes November general election from other contest dates.
- [Texas SOS — Canvass and Recount Deadline Advisory 2026-28](https://www.sos.texas.gov/elections/laws/advisory2026-28.shtml), October 23, 2026: verifies November 4 5 p.m. qualifying domestic ballot receipt, November 9 certain ballot corrections/overseas-military late arrivals, and distinct November 16 post-election ballot board timing. Its subject is federal/state/county contests.
- [Texas SOS — Advisory 2026-29, cities/schools and other political subdivisions](https://www.sos.texas.gov/elections/laws/advisory2026-29.shtml) separately verifies relevant November 9 cure/late-arrival framework for those elections.

## Comparison with current authority guide

The guide's framework paragraphs and FAQs correctly distinguish **2026-specific** October 23 ABBM application cutoff, early-voting dates, 7 p.m. November 3 domestic receipt, qualifying 5 p.m. November 4 postmark exception, November 9 in-person carrier-envelope correction, and overseas/military separate paths. This targeted pass finds **no change required** to these statements; the guide does not present October 23 as the last day to *return* an issued mail ballot or claim that all ballots can be returned as late as November 9.
