# Texas Public Information Act — 2025 board access and nonresponse remedies

**Primary legal-source check:** 2026-10-28. **Issue:** [#2938](https://github.com/keeptxred/texas-heartland-hub/issues/2938). **Scope:** new 2025 HB4219 Section 552.328, HB4310 Sections 552.401–.407, agency judicial challenges Sections 552.324–.325. This is a **bounded**, not all-claims-complete, Public Information Act review. Preserve original guide updated date 2026-09-06.

| Claim | Current official source | Checked and distinguished |
|---|---|---|
| When a body does not answer a records request | [Government Code §552.328(a)–(c)](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/GV/htm/GV.552.htm#552.328) (2025 HB4219 effective Sep 1, 2025) | Complaint includes original request and any body correspondence; **only after AG finding** of improper nonresponse, body must arrange public-information officer training within 6 months, may not charge that requester for this request and, if claiming withholding, must request AG ruling within 5 business days of AG notification and release unless compelling reason. Complaint alone doesn't waive substantive confidentiality. |
| Government tries to overturn AG disclosure decision | [§552.324(a)–(b), §552.325](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/GV/htm/GV.552.htm#552.324) | Its withholding suit goes against the **Attorney General**, in Travis County district court, generally within **30 calendar days after receiving** the decision, with special Section 552.353(b)(3) deadline for preserving particular officer defense; requester has notice and intervention rights. Distinct from any claim the requester may initiate. |
| Who has new board-member access rights | [§§552.401–.403](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/GV/htm/GV.552.htm#552.401) (2025 HB4310 effective Sep 1, 2025) | Defined members directing a governmental body or qualifying §552.371 nongovernmental entity, **acting in official capacity**, can inspect and/or duplicate public information promptly and **without charge**. No general public fee exemption; Subchapter K **does not apply to Legislature and specified legislative agencies** (§552.402). |
| How confidential and privileged material is treated | [§§552.403(c)–(d), 552.404–.407](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/GV/htm/GV.552.htm#552.403) | Member may request cost-free redaction of legally confidential content. Attorney-client privileged information is **not subject** to special disclosure unless relevant attorney-client relation includes member; entity gives notice of this withholding. §552.404 allows specifically described confidentiality agreement; §552.405 allows AG review of confidentiality, written decision by 45th **business** day and Travis County judicial appeal; §552.406 permits qualified member to pursue mandamus with venue rules. Other-law access preserved under §552.407. |
