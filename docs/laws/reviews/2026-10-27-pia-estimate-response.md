# Texas Public Information Act cost estimates — bounded primary-source review

**Checked:** October 27, 2026. **Master issue:** [#2938](https://github.com/keeptxred/texas-heartland-hub/issues/2938).

**Scope:** Reader guidance on PIA cost estimates, how a requester must answer an itemized statement, when an updated estimate is necessary, and separate withholding-ruling deadlines in `src/data/law-topic-open-records-authority.ts`. The full guide is **not** certified.

| Precise claim | Primary authority | Source finding and necessary qualification |
|---|---|---|
| When an agency must prepare an itemized estimate | [Texas Government Code §552.2615(a)](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/GetStatute.aspx?Code=GV&Value=552.2615); [Attorney General model](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/open-government/governmental-bodies/charges-public-information/public-information-cost-estimate-model) | If legally permissible **copy charges exceed $40** or covered paper-record inspection charges under §552.271 exceed $40, it must send a written itemized estimate identifying fees and, when available, a less-costly access method. Not a blanket assertion that agencies can charge $40 for every request. |
| Requester reply and automatic withdrawal | [§552.2615(b)](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/GetStatute.aspx?Code=GV&Value=552.2615) | Requester must **respond in writing** within **10 business days after the estimate was sent**, accepting charges, modifying request, or notifying the agency of a submitted Attorney General overcharge complaint. Absent response, the covered request is treated as withdrawn, subject to legally effective notices. |
| Updated estimate when projected charge rises | [§552.2615(c)-(d)](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/GetStatute.aspx?Code=GV&Value=552.2615) | If estimate rises by **20 percent or more** before information is provided, updated itemized estimate and reply obligations apply; statutory final-charge ceiling also applies. Do not conflate an estimate with a final assessment or presume every charge is legal. |
| Separate Attorney General withholding-ruling clock | [§552.2615(g); §552.301](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/GetStatute.aspx?Code=GV&Value=552.2615); [AG cost estimate form](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/open-government/governmental-bodies/charges-public-information/public-information-cost-estimate-model-form) | Section 552.2615 **does not extend agency Subchapter G timelines** for ruling requests and supporting materials. Do not mistake the requester's 10-business-day fee reply for agency's 10- and 15-business-day withholding-ruling obligations. |
